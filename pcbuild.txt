GPUs:
    Low (1080p)
        - intel arc b580
    Mid (1440p)
        - rx970
    High (4k)
        - rtx 5080
        - Radeon rx7900

Intel new to gpus so drivers are not great
AMD and NVIDIA good picks
*VRAM (memory embedded onto the gpu, at least 12)






CPUs:
    AMD (Ryzen), old AM4 (1000-5000 CPUs), new AM5 (7000-current)
    Intel old (LG700 12-14th gen), new (LGA 1851 Intel Core Ultra-current)
    *Core count (6 is min, 8 is recommended)
    *old cpus can outperform because single thread speeds are higher (#cores doesn't mean faster for games)

Motherboards:
    AMD, low -> high:
        - A -> B -> X
    Intel, low -> high:
        - H -> B -> Z
    *socket + chipset 
    *higher number after letter the more current






RAM:
    DDR4 - older (needs 16gb)
        Intel - LGA 1200, *1700
    DDR5 - current (needs 32gb)
        Intel - LGA 1851, 1954, *1700
        AMD - AM5
    *High speed to low CAS latency (5600mhz CL40 DDR5 is bad) (5600mhz CL30 DDR5 is good)

RAM kits:
    AM5 
        6000mhz CL30 DDR5
        6000mhz CL28 DDR5
    LGA 1851 
        6400mhz CL32 DDR5






SSDs:
*just more is better
*just make sure has DRAM controller (r/w speeds can fall off over time)







PSUs:
*Min PSU for GPU lookup (maybe add 100 watts buffer to be safe)
*at least 80 plus bronze (power prevention measures for gpu or cpu in power outage)
*modularity is power cables that you have access to (not bundled in the PSU)





Cases:
*match motherboard size with the case size (ATX, MicroATX, ITX)



Cooling:
*out of the box coolers for cpu aren't as good as after market
*AIO, water coolers, best, pumps can be loud
*air coolers, not far behind, last long



Case Fans:
*at least 4
